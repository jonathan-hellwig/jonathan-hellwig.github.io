Jonathan Hellwig

I am a Ph.D. researcher at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT) in Germany, affiliated with the Logic of Autonomous Dynamical Systems research group. My research focuses on formal verification of autonomous and cyber-physical systems, developing proof calculi for differential-algebraic systems and proof-producing pipelines that turn reachability results (such as zonotopes) into formal safety certificates.

Before starting my Ph.D. at KIT, I earned a degree in Industrial Mathematics. During my studies, I developed a strong interest in robotics and machine learning as a member of the RoboCup SPL team HULKs e.V., where I worked on localization, sensor fusion, and object detection.

If you would like to get in touch or explore potential collaborations, please feel free to reach out to me at jonathan[dot]hellwig[at]kit[dot]edu.
